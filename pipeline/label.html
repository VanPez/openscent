OpenScent · held-out evaluation

Label 50 sentences, blind

This is the only honest accuracy measurement the filter will get. Judge each sentence on its own merits — the filter's answers are in heldout-key.json and must stay unopened until you have finished.

Score once, then stop. If a filter rule is later changed because of what this set reveals, the set is burned and a new one has to be drawn. That is a DEVLOG entry, not a quiet re-score.

Keep

The sentence describes a smell and names a molecule that could be resolved to a structure. Both halves are required.
"3-(n-propyl)phenol has a leather-like, phenolic and ink-like odor."

Drop

Anything else — no real descriptor, no resolvable molecule, a test protocol, a claim, a malodour, or a smell attached to a mixture.
"This feedstock itself possesses an odour in the direction of grapefruit." — lovely, but a feedstock is never one structure.
