Keyboard: D H T M X classifies the selected row and advances, ↑ ↓ move, ⌫ clears.

D — descriptor

Says what it smells like. Ontology material.

H — hedonic

Pleasant / unpleasant. A judgement, not a smell.

T — tier

top / heart / base. Not per-compound assertable.

M — malodour

Deodorant vocabulary. Flags the document.

X — noise

Should have been stopped. Feeds the stop list.
